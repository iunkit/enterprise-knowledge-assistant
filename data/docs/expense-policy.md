# Expense Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees and contractors of Northwind Labs
who incur business expenses on the company's behalf.

## Submission deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date the expense
was incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except with
written approval from a department head.

## Approval thresholds

Expenses under 5,000 INR are auto-approved once submitted. Expenses between
5,000 and 50,000 INR require manager approval. Anything above 50,000 INR
requires both manager and finance approval before the expense is incurred, not
after.

## Travel

Domestic flights should be booked in economy class. Business class is permitted
only for flights longer than six hours. Hotel spend is capped at 8,000 INR per
night in tier-1 cities and 5,000 INR per night elsewhere.

Personal travel combined with a business trip is allowed, but only the business
portion is reimbursable, and the itinerary must be shared with finance before
booking.

## Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 2,000 INR per day. Alcohol is
not reimbursable under any circumstances, including client entertainment.

## Non-reimbursable items

Traffic fines, personal subscriptions, gym memberships, and lost personal
property are not reimbursable.
