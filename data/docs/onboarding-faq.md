# New Joiner FAQ

## When do I get my laptop?

IT ships hardware to arrive on or before day one. If it has not arrived by day
two, raise a ticket in the IT portal and tag it "onboarding-blocked" — those
tickets are picked up the same day.

## What is the probation period?

Probation is six months for full-time employees and three months for those
joining from an internship. A formal review takes place at the halfway point and
at the end of probation.

## How does leave work?

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing
at two days per month. Up to 10 unused days carry over into the following year;
anything beyond that lapses on 31 March.

Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, and a medical certificate is required
for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## What are the working hours?

Core hours are 11:00 to 17:00 IST, during which everyone is expected to be
available. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible. Teams working with US
customers may agree a shifted schedule with their manager.

## Who approves my leave?

Your reporting manager. Requests for more than five consecutive days should be
raised at least two weeks in advance.

## Where do I find the org chart?

The org chart lives in the HR portal under "Company". It is regenerated nightly
from the HR system.
